Questions

Before you decide.

What do I need to get started?

On a plan with AI included, two things:

  • A spare SIM. A new number for her WhatsApp, in any spare phone that you keep charged and online. Never your main business line.
  • About ten minutes. We set her up with you on our servers, and your team opens her dashboard in a browser.

For the free trial, and on the Own AI key plan, she runs on a computer of yours, so you also need a Windows 10 or 11 PC, or a Mac with macOS 13 or newer, that can stay on all day. The Own AI key plan also needs an Anthropic API key with billing switched on (a chat subscription is not the same thing and does not work).

AI included, or my own AI key: what is the difference?

With AI included there is nothing to set up and nothing to install: we run her on our servers, and she works within a fair monthly allowance. If your company already has an Anthropic API account, the Own AI key plan costs less: you install her on one Windows PC or Mac in your office, connect your key, and Anthropic bills you directly for what she uses.

If you are not sure, choose a plan with AI included.

How does the free trial work?

You get the full product for 14 days, on your own computer. There is no payment first. We e-mail you a trial key and the download links, and you install her on a Windows PC or Mac that can stay on.

When the 14 days are over she pauses until you buy a plan. Nothing is deleted: her notes, your documents and your team list stay on your computer.

When you buy a plan with AI included, we move her to our servers. We write to you to do it, and what she learnt in the trial can come with her.

How do the plans on our servers work?

On every plan with AI included we run Myra for you, on our servers. We set her up with you, keep her updated and backed up, and your team opens her dashboard in a browser, at her own address. There is nothing to install and no computer of yours to keep switched on.

What is different from a computer in your office, said plainly:

  • Your company’s chats, files and everything she knows are stored on our servers instead of a computer of yours.
  • Our support staff can sign in to her dashboard to help. Every such sign-in shows in your activity, and you can switch it off.
  • A website’s “I am not a robot” check cannot be handed to a person there, so she cannot finish a site that insists on one.

She still needs a WhatsApp number of her own: a new SIM in any spare phone, kept charged and online at least every few days. You link it by scanning a code on her dashboard. After you pay, we e-mail you her address, usually within one working day. If you tried her on your own computer, we write to you to move her, and what she learnt can come with her.

What does the monthly allowance mean, and what if we use more?

Each plan with AI included says about how many replies and website tasks a month of her AI covers. These are fair-use estimates, not hard counts of messages.

When a month’s allowance is used up she says so in plain words and pauses her AI work until the next month, unless you add a top-up or move up a plan. On the Own AI key plan there is no monthly limit from us: you pay your AI provider for what she uses.

How do I pay?

By UPI or card. Payments are handled by Razorpay, on its own page: your card or UPI details are entered there, not on this website.

A monthly plan is paid each month from a payment link we e-mail you a few days before it is due; nothing is taken from your account automatically. A yearly plan is paid once a year and costs less than twelve monthly payments; each plan shows the saving. Prices are in rupees, plus GST where it applies. Give your GST number when you buy and it goes on your invoice.

The Own AI key plan can also be paid for once: ₹3,99,000, plus GST where it applies. That licence does not expire. Support and updates are included for one year from the day you pay; after that year she keeps working.

Can I cancel?

Yes, at any time. She keeps working until the end of the period you have paid for, and then pauses. On a plan with AI included, download a copy of her data from her dashboard, or ask us for one, before the end; on the Own AI key plan your data stays on your computer. A licence paid for once has nothing to cancel: it never renews and takes no further payment. The details are on the Refunds and cancellation page.

Where is my data?

On a plan with AI included, on our servers, because that is where she runs: every message she has seen, your team list, her library of your documents, her notes. Our support staff can sign in to her dashboard to help, every such sign-in shows in your activity, and you can switch it off.

During the free trial and on the Own AI key plan, all of that stays on your own computer, and none of it is stored with us. Our server keeps your licence, a code for the computer it runs on, and your invoices.

When she thinks, the text of that one conversation is sent to the AI provider and the answer comes back. The Privacy page says exactly what goes where.

Which countries do you sell in?

We are starting with India: prices are in rupees and payments run through Razorpay. If your company is somewhere else, talk to us before you buy.

What does one plan cover?

One digital employee, with one WhatsApp number of her own. On a plan with AI included she runs on our servers, so there is no computer of yours to count. On the Own AI key plan she runs on one office computer with one licence key; if you replace that computer, release the licence on the old one and enter the key on the new one.
