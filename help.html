Before you buy

What is Work Sailor?

A digital employee for your company. Her name is Myra unless you give her another. She lives on WhatsApp, on a number that belongs to your company, and works from our servers, or, on the Own AI key plan, from one computer in your office.

She reads and answers your team’s messages, learns your company from the documents and chats she is given, keeps reminders and to-do lists, writes documents in your formats, runs the routines you give her, and does tasks on websites. She never changes anything outside her own notes without a yes from someone allowed to give it.

Who is she for?

Owners and managers of small and mid-sized companies that already run on WhatsApp. You do not need to be technical. If you can use WhatsApp and open a page in a browser, you can work with her, and we set her up with you.

Is this an official WhatsApp product?

No. Work Sailor is an independent product. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by WhatsApp.

She uses WhatsApp the way WhatsApp on a computer does: as a linked device of a number, by scanning a code. WhatsApp can restrict a number that a program uses. That is why she must have a number of her own, and why we ask you never to link your main business line.

Why does she need a number of her own?

So that your business number is never at risk. If WhatsApp ever restricts her number, you lose a SIM, not your business. Your data is not tied to the number: it stays where she runs, and you link a new number by scanning a code again.

A number of her own also makes her a colleague people can message directly, and add to a group, like anyone else in the team.

Which languages does she work in?

English, Hindi and Hinglish. She answers in the language you wrote in, and you can tell her which one you prefer.

Can I give her another name?

Yes. Myra is only the default. During setup you can name her, say how formal she should be, which language she should prefer and how she signs off. You can change all of it later.

What does she cost, and can I try her first?

The plans and prices are on the Pricing page. The Own AI key plan can also be paid for once, and that licence does not expire. You can start with a 14-day free trial of the full product, with no payment first. When the trial is over she pauses until you buy a plan, and nothing is deleted. The trial runs on your own computer; when you buy a plan with AI included, we move her to our servers, and we write to you to do it.

Where does she run? Do we have to install anything?

On a plan with AI included, she runs on our servers and there is nothing to install. We set her up with you and keep her updated and backed up. Your team opens her dashboard in a browser, at an address we send you. You still give her a WhatsApp number of her own and link it by scanning a code.

What is different, said plainly: your company’s chats, files and everything she knows are stored on our servers instead of a computer of yours. Our support staff can sign in to her dashboard to help; every such sign-in shows in your activity, and you can switch it off. And a website’s “I am not a robot” check cannot be handed to a person there, so she cannot finish a site that insists on one.

Only the free trial and the Own AI key plan are installed, on a Windows PC or Mac of yours that stays on. The prices are on the Pricing page.

AI included, or my own AI key: what is the difference?

With AI included there is nothing to set up and nothing to install: we run her on our servers, and she works within a fair monthly allowance. If your company already has an Anthropic API account, the Own AI key plan costs less: you install her on one Windows PC or Mac in your office, connect your key, and Anthropic bills you directly for what she uses.

If you are not sure, choose a plan with AI included.

Setting her up

What do I need?

On a plan with AI included:

  • A new SIM for her WhatsApp number, in any spare phone that you keep charged and online
  • About ten minutes, with us: we set her up on our servers, and you open her dashboard in a browser

For the free trial and the Own AI key plan, she runs on a computer of yours, so you also need:

  • A Windows 10 or 11 PC, or a Mac with macOS 13 or newer, that can stay on all day
  • Your licence key, which we send by e-mail
  • On the Own AI key plan only: an Anthropic API key with billing switched on
How long does the setup take, and what are the steps?

About ten minutes. Her dashboard walks you through it and ticks each step as it is done. If you leave in the middle, it opens where you stopped.

  1. LicenceOn your own computer, enter your key once; on our servers there is no key to enter. On the Own AI key plan, paste your AI key next; the app checks it on the spot.
  2. WhatsAppScan the code with her phone: WhatsApp, Settings, Linked devices, Link a device.
  3. YouYour name and number, so she knows who the owner is. Alerts and approvals come to you.
  4. Your employeeHer name, her style, the language she prefers.
  5. Optional, now or laterWorking hours, the groups she is part of, your documents, a Google connection, website logins.
Do I have to install her myself?

On a plan with AI included there is nothing to install: we set her up on our servers with you. For the free trial and the Own AI key plan it is an installer like any other program. If you would like company, talk to us and we set her up with you on a call.

I tried her on my computer. What happens when we buy?

When you buy a plan with AI included, we write to you and move her to our servers. She keeps working on your computer until then. What she learnt in the trial can come with her: you download a backup on your computer and restore it on her new dashboard, and we help you with it. She can work from one place at a time, so the copy on your computer stops when she moves.

On the Own AI key plan nothing moves: she stays on your computer.

What should I send her in the first week?

Nothing is required to start. The more she gets in the first week, the better her first answers are. Send it in a direct message, drop it in a group she is in, or add it on the dashboard. Any format: Word, Excel, PowerPoint, PDF, pictures, Google Docs links, or just typed.

  • The team list. Who, their role, their area, who approves what
  • Your policies and ways of working, in whatever form they exist
  • Price lists and rate cards, so a quote carries the right numbers the first time
  • Your formats. A past proposal, quote, invoice, offer letter, report
  • Three or four routines, in plain words. These become standing orders
  • Working hours and days off
  • Which groups she should be in, and what each is for

In the first week we also set up your first standing orders with you, from the routines you send.

How do I know it worked?

Send her a direct message that says “hi”. She answers with her name and asks what you would like to start with.

Can I move her to another computer?

On our servers there is nothing to move: she is not tied to a computer of yours. On the Own AI key plan, yes. One key works on one machine at a time. On the old machine, open the Licence page and press “Release from this machine” (or ask us), then enter the key on the new one. Never run her on two machines at once.

Working with her

How do I talk to her?

In a direct message: just write. She answers anyone in your team list.

In a group: start the message with her name, mention her, or reply to one of her messages. Everything else in the group she reads quietly for context and does not answer. Several messages in a row from you get one reply, not five.

You can also send voice notes, pictures, documents and links. The audio of a voice note is turned into text where she runs, and is not sent anywhere else.

What does she do on her own, and what waits for a yes?

On her own: reading, answering within what a person may know, keeping notes, drafting, reminding a team member in a direct message, filing documents, the morning plan and the evening roundup.

Always with a yes first: anything that reaches a client or anyone outside the team; any message to a group; creating a group, adding or removing people, renaming it; sending a document outside the team; anything involving money.

How does saying yes work?

She tells you exactly what will happen and gives the request a short code, for example: “I will send the proposal (4 pages) to Rohan Mehta. Reply yes K7Q2 to go ahead, or no K7Q2 to cancel.”

Someone allowed to approve replies yes K7Q2. Hindi works too: haan K7Q2, nahi K7Q2. Open requests are also on the dashboard, with Approve and Reject buttons.

Only people you marked as approvers count. A request nobody answers goes to the next approver after half an hour and expires after a day.

How do I teach her something, or correct her?

Tell her: “our refund window is 48 hours”. She keeps it, dated and with your name on it. If she gets something wrong, say so in the same chat: “that’s wrong, the GST rate on workshops is 18%”. She fixes the note and says what she changed.

Everything she has been taught is on the dashboard, each note with who said it, when, and a remove button.

What is a standing order?

A job you describe once and she does every time, the same way: “Every Monday at 9 am, send me the weekend summary.” She turns your words into a plan, shows you the plan, and saves it only when you say yes. Every run is written down: what started it, what each step did, who approved, what went out.

How do I stop her?

Tell her “stop your own work”, or use the same switch on the dashboard. Every standing order pauses and nothing of her own starts until you tell her to carry on. She still answers people.

What can she not do?

Voice and video calls (she can make a call link for you, but she cannot speak in a call), payments inside WhatsApp, live location and broadcast lists. WhatsApp does not allow these from a linked device. She says so plainly when asked.

When something stops

She has stopped answering. What do I check?
  1. Is WhatsApp linked?The dashboard says so on its first page. If not, link her again (the next question).
  2. Is her phone charged and online?If it stays offline too long, WhatsApp unlinks her.
  3. On your own computer: is it on and awake, with internet?If it sleeps, she stops. A laptop closed on the desk is asleep. On our servers this does not apply.
  4. Is the allowance used up?She tells you when it is, and when she will be back.

She alerts you on WhatsApp when something is wrong, with what happened and what to do. A short “Fixed” follows when it is over.

WhatsApp has unlinked her. How do I link her again?

Open the dashboard, go to WhatsApp and press the button to link again. On her phone open WhatsApp, then Settings, Linked devices, Link a device, and scan the code. Nothing else changes: her notes, documents, team list and standing orders are all still there.

To keep the link healthy: her phone switched on and online at least every few days, the dedicated number only, and never the same number on a second machine.

How do I know if the problem is on your side?

Our status page says whether licence checks, her AI, the servers she runs on and buying are working. When something is wrong on our side it says so there, with what we are doing about it and when it is fixed.

What happens if our internet goes down?

On a plan with AI included she runs on our servers, so an outage in your office does not stop her: anyone who can reach WhatsApp can still reach her. Her phone needs to come online again within a few days.

On your own computer she cannot read or send on WhatsApp until it is back. Her licence keeps working for 24 hours without reaching our server, then she pauses until it can. The moment it answers again she carries on by herself, even after a long weekend with the machine off.

What does “licence suspended” mean?

The licence has been switched off: a payment did not go through, the licence was used on a second machine, or the app’s own protection noticed that its files were changed or copied. She stops. Nothing is deleted: your data stays where it is.

Talk to us. If it was a false alarm, we switch the licence back on and nothing is lost.

Your data

Where does our data live?

On a plan with AI included, on our servers, because that is where she runs: every message she has seen, the link to WhatsApp, your team list, her library of your documents, her notes, the pictures and files. Our support staff can sign in to her dashboard to help. Every such sign-in shows in your activity, and you can switch it off on her Settings page.

During the free trial and on the Own AI key plan, all of that stays on your own computer. None of it is stored with us.

What does your server know about us?

Your licence and your invoices. For a copy on your own computer, also the computer it is bound to (a code worked out from the machine, never anything personal), the times the app checked in, and any protection report. On a plan with AI included it also counts how much AI work was used, never the words, and our servers hold everything she works with, as the answer above says.

What does the AI provider see?

When she thinks, the text of that one conversation is sent to the AI provider (Anthropic) and the answer comes back. On the Own AI key plan it goes from your computer straight to your own Anthropic account. During the free trial and on a plan with AI included it passes through our licence server on the way, and the licence server keeps none of it.

She never writes down phone numbers, e-mail addresses, one-time codes, passwords or bank details. The Privacy page has the full list of what goes where.

Is there a backup?

On a plan with AI included we keep her backups on our servers for you, and you can download a copy from her dashboard at any time.

On your own computer, a copy of her database is saved there every night, and the newest 14 are kept. You can name a second folder that gets a copy of each one: a folder that your cloud drive syncs works well.

How do we delete everything?

Write to us. Her data is wiped where she runs (on our servers, with its backups, or her data folder on your own computer), the licence is closed, and we remove every record about your company from our side within 30 days and confirm it in writing. Invoices are kept for as long as tax law requires, with the amounts and your company name only.
