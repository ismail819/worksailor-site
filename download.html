Before you install

What to have ready.

  • A computer that can stay on all day. A desktop or a mini PC is best. A laptop works with the lid open, plugged in, and sleep switched off.
  • A new SIM for her WhatsApp number. Put it in any spare phone and set up WhatsApp on it. Never use your main business line or anyone’s personal number.
  • Your licence key. It comes by e-mail when you start a trial or buy a plan. One key works on one machine.
  • On the Own AI key plan only: an Anthropic API key with billing switched on. A chat subscription is not the same thing and does not work.
  • About ten minutes.

Why her own number

She must never share your main line.

WhatsApp can restrict a number that a program uses. If that ever happens to her number, you lose a SIM, not your business. Your data is not tied to the number: it stays on your machine, and you link a new number by scanning a code again.

Her phone can go in a drawer once she is linked. Keep it charged, switched on and online at least every few days, or WhatsApp unlinks her.

The first three steps

From download to her first answer.

  1. Install and enter your key

    Open the installer like any other program. The app asks for your licence key once. On the Own AI key plan it then asks for your AI key and checks it on the spot.

  2. Link her WhatsApp number

    The app shows a code. On her phone open WhatsApp, then Settings, Linked devices, Link a device, and scan it.

  3. Send her how you work

    Tell her who you are, give her a name if you want another, choose the groups she is part of, and send your team list and documents. Then message her “hi”.

She answers with her name and asks what you would like to start with. That is how you know it worked. If you would like company for the setup, talk to us and we do it with you on a call.

Keep her machine awake

If the machine sleeps, she stops.

No answers, no reminders, no standing orders until it wakes. Two settings prevent it. The screen may go off; that is fine.

On Windows

Settings, System, Power: set sleep to Never when plugged in. Let the PC sign in by itself after a restart, so she comes back without anyone touching it.

On a Mac

System Settings, Energy: switch on “Prevent automatic sleeping when the display is off” and “Start up automatically after a power failure”. A Mac closed on the desk is asleep.
