Draft: to be reviewed by a lawyer before launch.This page describes, in plain words, what the product really does with data. It is not final, and parts of it will change after legal review.

Version: draft-1

In short

  • On a plan with AI included we run her on our servers, so your company’s data is stored there. Section 4 says what that means.
  • During the free trial and on the Own AI key plan your company’s data lives on your own computer. None of your conversations or documents are stored with us.
  • Our licence server checks your licence. It knows the licence, your invoices and, for a copy on your own computer, a code for that machine.
  • When she thinks, the text of that one conversation is sent to the AI provider and the answer comes back.
  • We do not sell your data, share it, or use it to train AI models.
  • This website uses no cookies and no trackers.

1. Who is responsible for what

Work Sailor is provided by [the company’s legal name, to be added], [registered address, to be added].

Your company decides what she does and whose messages she handles: your team’s, and those of the clients, vendors and others who write to her number. As we understand India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, that makes your company the one responsible for that personal data, and us the provider of the software and of the licence service. [Roles to be confirmed by the lawyer; a data-processing agreement will be added.]

2. What stays on your own computer

During the free trial and on the Own AI key plan, she runs on a computer of yours, and this stays on it. On a plan with AI included the same things are kept on our servers instead (section 4).

  • Every message she has seen, in the chats she is in
  • The link to WhatsApp
  • Your team list: names, numbers, roles, areas
  • Her library of your documents, her notes and her notebooks
  • Pictures, voice notes and files. A voice note is turned into text on that computer; the audio never leaves it
  • Saved website logins. A password can be typed in, never read back
  • Nightly backups of her database, and a copy in a second folder if you name one

On your own computer none of this is stored with us. Phone numbers are never shown in full, even on her own dashboard.

What she refuses to keep. She never keeps identity documents, payslips, CVs or medical files, even if they are sent to her. She never writes down phone numbers, e-mail addresses, one-time codes, passwords, bank details, Aadhaar, PAN or GST numbers.

Encryption, said honestly. Her database can be kept encrypted, so that it cannot be read without a valid licence even by someone who copies the folder. We switch this on once it has been tried on your kind of machine; ask us whether it is on for yours. The link to WhatsApp, the library’s files and the media are ordinary files in the app’s folder for now. Protect the machine the way you protect your other company files.

5. What goes to the AI provider

Her thinking is done by an AI model from Anthropic. Each time she thinks, the text she needs for that one conversation is sent and the answer comes back: the messages of that conversation, the parts of your documents and notes that her search found for it, and a picture or a document’s text when she is asked to read one.

  • On the Own AI key plan it goes from your computer straight to Anthropic, under your own account and Anthropic’s terms. It does not pass through us.
  • During the free trial and on plans with AI included it passes through our licence server on its way to Anthropic. The licence server holds it only for the length of that request and keeps no copy of the words. (On plans with AI included the conversation itself is kept on the servers she runs on: section 4.)

What the AI provider keeps, and for how long, is set by its own terms. [The provider’s current retention terms to be checked and stated here.] The provider’s servers may be outside India.

6. Other services she uses for you

  • WhatsApp. She sends and receives on your number like any linked device. WhatsApp’s own terms and privacy policy apply to that number.
  • Google, if you connect it. She reads Google Docs, Sheets, Slides and Drive files through your own Google sign-in, so she sees only what that account can see. With the permissions you give at sign-in she also adds rows and changes cells in Sheets, and reads and adds to the calendars that account can see. She never shares or deletes a file.
  • Your company mailbox, if you connect it. She looks at new mail for the standing orders that need it.
  • Web pages and websites. To read a link she fetches the page without signing in, and never opens a page that asks for a password. On plans with AI included, the page is fetched through our server. Website tasks run in a browser of her own where she runs (on our servers, or on your own computer), with the logins you saved for her. When she looks something up on the web, the search is run by the AI provider, which receives the words searched for; you can switch this off in Settings.
  • Payments. Razorpay handles payments on its own page. Your card or UPI details are entered there, not on this website. We receive the result of the payment and what we need for your invoice.

7. What we do not do

  • We do not sell or share your data.
  • We do not use it to train AI models.
  • We do not read or keep your conversations. Our support sees your text only if you send it to us yourself, for example in an e-mail asking for help. On a plan with AI included your conversations are kept on our servers because she runs there, and our support sees them only during a sign-in that shows in your activity (section 4).

8. This website

  • No cookies, no trackers, no analytics, and nothing loaded from another company’s servers.
  • The trial and buy forms send what you type to our server, and nowhere else.
  • While you are buying, the reference of that purchase is kept in your browser tab so the thank-you page can tell you how the payment went. It is gone when you close the tab.
  • The company that hosts this website may keep ordinary server logs: the address a request came from, the time, the page.

9. The people who write to her

She reads the chats she is in. Tell your team, and the groups you put her in, that a digital employee reads those chats. If a client asks your company to forget them, that request is yours to fulfil: the dashboard lets the owner remove notes and documents.

Work Sailor is for businesses. It is not meant for handling children’s personal data.

10. Deleting your data

Write to us. Her data is wiped where she runs (on our servers, with its backups, or her data folder on your own computer), the licence is closed, and we remove every record about your company from our side within 30 days and confirm it to you in writing. When she runs on your own computer there is no copy of your conversations on our side to delete. Invoices are kept for as long as tax law requires, with the amounts and your company name only.

What the AI provider keeps of your conversations is governed by its own terms and, on the Own AI key plan, by your own account with it.

11. An honest limit

On a plan with AI included she is as safe as our servers and as the passwords of the people who sign in to her dashboard. [How the servers are protected to be described here before launch.]

On your own computer she is as safe as that computer. If it is stolen while the app is active, or someone else can sign in to it, they can reach what she knows. Lock it, and tell us at once if it is lost: we suspend the licence, and she stops.
