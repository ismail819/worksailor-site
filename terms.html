These terms cover the app, whether we run it for you or you install it, the licence service that lets it run, and this website. By starting a free trial or buying a plan you agree to them, together with the Privacy page and the Refunds and cancellation page.

2. The product, in short

On a plan with AI included we run the program for you on our servers (section 4). During the free trial and on the Own AI key plan you install it on one computer of yours. Either way you link it to a WhatsApp number of yours by scanning a code. The program is a digital employee: she reads and answers messages on that number from your team, in direct messages and in the groups you choose, and gives strangers a short polite reply. She reads the documents, pictures, voice notes and links your team sends her, keeps notes about your business, writes documents, runs the routines you define, and does tasks on websites in a browser of her own.

Anything that reaches someone outside your team, any message to a group, and anything involving money waits for a named person at your company to approve it.

3. Your licence

  • One licence, one machine. On a computer of yours, a licence key works on one computer at a time. To move, release it on the old machine and enter it on the new one, or ask us.
  • A right to use, not ownership. While your trial or your subscription runs, or for as long as a licence paid for once stays valid, you may use the app for your own company’s work. The software, its design, its name and its marks stay ours.
  • No copying or tampering. You may not copy the app, take it apart, change its files, or try to run one licence on more than one machine.
  • Automatic suspension. If the app’s files are changed, if its data folder is copied to another machine, or if a licence is used from a second machine, the licence is suspended automatically and she stops. A suspension deletes nothing. If it was a false alarm, we switch the licence back on.
  • The licence check. The app checks its licence with our server every 15 minutes. Without a connection it keeps working for 24 hours on its last check, and then pauses until it can reach the server again.

5. Her WhatsApp number

  • A number of her own is a condition of the licence. You must use a number obtained for this purpose. You must not link your main business number or anyone’s personal number.
  • WhatsApp may restrict the number. Work Sailor is an independent product. It links to WhatsApp as a linked device and is not an official WhatsApp product. WhatsApp can restrict or ban a number that a program uses. This is outside our control. It is not a defect of the product, and it is not a reason for a refund of a period already used.
  • What we do if it happens. We help you link a new number. Your data is untouched: it is where she runs (on our servers, or on your own computer) and is not tied to the number.
  • Your side of WhatsApp’s terms is yours. You are responsible for how your number is used under WhatsApp’s own terms. We do not act for you towards WhatsApp.

6. The free trial

A trial is the full product for 14 days. There is no payment first. The trial always runs on a computer of yours. When the trial is over she pauses until you buy a plan. Nothing is deleted when she pauses: your data stays on that computer. If you then buy a plan with AI included, we move her to our servers (section 4).

8. Her AI, and the monthly allowance

  • Plans with AI included. Her AI is part of the plan, with a monthly allowance. The figures shown for each plan (about so many replies, about so many website tasks) are fair-use estimates, not hard counts of messages. When a month’s allowance is used up she says so and pauses her AI work until the next month, unless you add a top-up or move up a plan.
  • The Own AI key plan. You connect your own account with the AI provider (Anthropic). The provider bills you directly for what she uses, under its own terms. We do not see that bill and we set no monthly limit.
  • The AI model is made by another company. It can be wrong. That is why she says where an answer came from, and why a person approves what leaves the team.

10. What she must not be used for

  • Bulk messaging, or spam of any kind
  • Messaging people who have not written to your company and are not in your team or your groups
  • Anything unlawful
  • Handling children’s personal data

Breaking these rules is a reason for us to suspend the licence.

11. Your data stays yours

Your messages, your documents, everything she writes for you and the notes she keeps about your business are yours. On a plan with AI included they are stored on our servers (section 4). During the free trial and on the Own AI key plan they live on your own computer, not with us. The Privacy page says exactly what our servers hold and what goes to the AI provider. When a subscription on your own computer ends, the data folder stays there until you delete it; when a plan on our servers ends, section 4 applies.
