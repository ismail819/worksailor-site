She answers the team

Ask her the way you would ask a colleague.

In a direct message or in a group, your team asks and she answers from your price lists, policies and past work, and says where the answer came from. If she does not have a number, she says so and asks. She never guesses.

  • Listens to voice notes; reads pictures, PDFs, Word, Excel and PowerPoint files
  • Answers in English, Hindi or Hinglish: the language you wrote in
  • Each person hears only what they are allowed to know

You stay in charge

Nothing leaves the team without a yes.

A message to a client, a post in a group, a payment, a change on a website: she prepares it, tells you exactly what will happen, and waits. Reply yes with the short code on WhatsApp, or press Approve on the dashboard.

  • Only the people you choose can say yes
  • One code for one action, never a bundle
  • A request nobody answers expires after a day

Standing orders

Tell her once. She does it every time.

Describe a routine in plain words. She turns it into a plan, shows you the plan, and saves it when you say yes. After that she does it the same way every time, and every run is written down.

  • “Every Monday at 9 am, send me last week’s summary.”
  • “If a lead has had no answer for two hours, nudge the front desk.”
  • “When an e-mail with an invoice arrives, file it and tell me.”

Websites

She gets things done on websites, in a browser of her own.

Download an invoice from a portal, fill in a form, check whether a date is free. She works in her own browser on your office machine, and you can watch every step. When a site asks for a one-time code, she asks a person on WhatsApp.

  • Signs in only with the logins you saved for her
  • A click that cannot be undone waits for a yes
  • You decide which sites she may use, and who may ask her

How it works

Working in about ten minutes.

Three steps, no technical knowledge. The app walks you through each one.

  1. Install the app

    On a Windows PC or a Mac in your office that can stay on all day. Enter the licence key we e-mail you.

  2. Link her WhatsApp number

    Give her a number of her own: a new SIM in any spare phone. Scan one code and she is on WhatsApp.

  3. Send her how you work

    Your team list, price lists, a past quote, the routines you repeat. Send them on WhatsApp or add them on the dashboard.

Safety and privacy

Your company’s data stays yours.

She is built the way you would want a new colleague to behave: careful with what she knows, and asking before she acts.

It stays on your machine

Messages, documents, her notes and your team list live on the computer in your office. None of it is stored with us, unless you choose to have us run her on the Managed plan.

She asks first

Anything that reaches a client, a group or a website’s final button waits for a yes from a person you named.

Each person, their own view

Money answers go only to people marked for finance, in a direct message. A document is never quoted to someone below its level.

What she will not do

She never keeps identity documents, payslips, CVs or medical files, and never writes down passwords, one-time codes or bank details.

When she thinks, the text of that one conversation is sent to the AI provider and the answer comes back. Nothing of your conversations is stored with us. Good to know before you start

Three things she needs from you.

A number of her own

A new SIM, never your main business line or anyone’s personal WhatsApp. WhatsApp can restrict a number that a program uses; if that ever happens you lose a SIM, not your business.

A machine that stays on

She works from one computer in your office. While it is on she works at any hour. If it sleeps, she stops until it wakes.

A little of your time in week one

She is better at what she has been shown than at what she has to guess. The first week is for sending her how you work.

Give her a desk for 14 days.

The full product, free for 14 days. No payment first. If you do not buy a plan she pauses, and nothing is deleted.
